Turn local recordings into reviewed vertical clips whose captions stay aligned after cuts, and give EmberBanter a repeatable first-show workflow. This expands a README-only workspace with recovered scenes/core production tools, optional caption burn-in, a local transcription benchmark, rehearsal scripts, platform research and measurement templates.

The clip tool produces up to three local 1080×1920 exports from actual in/out seconds. It preserves the original video/SRT, selects an explicit audio stream, keeps the full game frame by default, refuses unreviewed cuts/captions and existing output folders, and verifies the encoded files. Optional reviewed source-timeline SRT is clipped, rebased, burned into the MP4 and exported as a per-clip sidecar. Transcription produces a draft for human correction; finished audio mixing and listening review remain editing tasks. The tools perform no recording, upload or broadcast.

Validation: all 15 tests passed on Linux and DTG/Windows, including real exports, both layouts, audio-track selection, caption pixels in decoded frames, cut-relative subtitle timing, source preservation, review/output protection, word-error arithmetic and Windows PowerShell UTF-8 JSON handling. A CPU transcription trial processed 16.6 seconds of synthetic speech in 3.4 seconds with zero normalized edits across 38 reference words; that is not a real-voice accuracy estimate. The resulting SRT completed a captioned video export and full decode. Separate Windows launchers choose the checked OBS installation and the verified portable Shotcut copy. Details are in the dated caption handoff.

External mic/interface, Xbox capture, BRB silence, upload performance, owned channels and a real five-minute recording are unverified. Synthetic speech checks do not establish real recording accuracy. No public stream, published clip, music import or account enrollment occurred.
