# Clips and growth

Read [brand/state](../../references/brand-and-state.md). Use actual recording/transcript timestamps, or label proposed moments as untimed concepts. Never claim a rendered clip, transcription or upload unless the tool produced it. Preserve context and avoid misleading cuts or fabricated quotes.

Select up to three moments with a clear opening, understandable context and payoff. Produce hook, accurate cut boundaries if available, caption draft, title alternatives and a simple thumbnail brief. Keep captions readable on phones; leave space for platform controls. Verify current destination limits and encoding before export.

Prioritize a sustainable loop: one session, a few worthwhile clips, review performance, adjust the next session. Track actual views, retention, unique chatters, followers and average viewers when available. Use unknown for missing values; explain the denominator for rates and avoid predicting earnings from invented audiences.

Use the local source repository's session-log template when creating a tracker. For a transparent editorial rubric, opening experiments or revenue arithmetic, route to [growth and economics](../emberbanter-growth-economics/SKILL.md). The bundled [clip tool](../emberbanter-engineering/scripts/clip_tools.py) supports reviewed source-timeline SRT captions and local vertical exports; generated transcripts still need correction. Research current platform behavior before making recommendations that depend on it. Do not buy followers, spam communities or mass-message people. Publishing and schedule changes need the owner's request.

Return ready-to-review clip concepts/copy or the actual rendered outputs, plus the one strongest improvement supported by evidence.
