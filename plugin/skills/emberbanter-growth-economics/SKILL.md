# Growth and economics

Read [verified state](../../references/brand-and-state.md), then the relevant part of [growth playbook](../../references/growth-playbook.md) or [money and platform sources](../../references/monetization-and-sources.md). Research current platform rules before quoting them. Never promise virality, a proprietary ranking formula, audience growth or income.

Make one repeatable show promise. Build an opening, stakes, audience choice, escalation, payoff and next-episode reason. Supply usable host lines and quiet-chat fallbacks. Match the owner's available time; a suggested calendar is not a scheduled automation. Use the proposed [series and experiments](../../references/series-and-experiments.md).

Keep facts, editorial judgment and synthetic examples visibly separate. Use actual exported analytics and timestamps when available. Missing audience, eligibility, payout and cost values are unknown, never zero. Do not equate Shorts views with live viewers, sum cross-platform audiences as unique people, or treat likes as proof of satisfaction. Compare the same format, traffic source and observation age.

For arithmetic, run [studio_strategy.py](scripts/studio_strategy.py) with an explicit input file; examples are [hypothetical only](../../assets/examples/economics.example.json). The calculator accepts disjoint net receipts in one currency/period. YouTube aggregate RPM can already include memberships and Supers: use ad-only net revenue per the matching view denominator, then add other distinct receipts once. Do not substitute advertiser CPM. Explain costs, unpaid work and tax exclusions. Membership targets are conditional arithmetic, not a prediction that people will subscribe.

Clip scoring uses a disclosed subjective rubric, not a platform algorithm. A score never overrides context, rights, accurate captions or usable sound. Without media, return untimed concepts. For real cuts route to the clips skill and bundled engineering tools.

Deliver one concrete experiment, a measurable review rule, a realistic workload and a clearly labeled financial scenario if requested. Keep public posting, paid accounts, sponsor outreach and recurring scheduling within explicit authorization. Do not fabricate contacts, endorsements or measured creator performance.
