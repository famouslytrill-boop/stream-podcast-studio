## Reusable kit specification

- Avatar: square master, centered monogram, generous safe area; test a 48-pixel crop. Keep the existing working badge until the owner chooses otherwise.
- Typography: bold uppercase sans-serif for short headings; `system-ui, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif` for readable body/captions. Use installed fonts/fallbacks; no new font license or proprietary font file is bundled. Preview lettering is art direction, not a reusable font file.
- Landscape scenes: 1920×1080 Starting, Gameplay, Talk, BRB and Ending; preserve game visibility. Existing HTML scene kit remains EmberBanter.
- Vertical clips: 1080×1920; high-contrast captions over a dark backing, roughly two short lines; avoid destination UI areas and preview on a phone. Crop only when the relevant action remains visible.
- Thumbnails: 1280×720 working canvas, one event/object, a brief honest phrase, strong separation; no fake results, faces or analytics.
- Podcast cover: square 3000×3000 production master, readable name at small scale, optional episode art variants. Verify the destination's current technical rules before submission; a master canvas is not a submitted cover.
- Motion proposal: 200–350 ms restrained wipes or ember/pulse details; avoid flashing. Respect reduced-motion settings in reusable web layouts.
- Original sonic proposal: a short three-note motif E4–G4–B4 with a soft low hit, under two seconds, recorded/designed originally. This is a composition direction, not a delivered audio file; do not sample a game's theme or an identifiable creator sound.
